TMPlayer's libav.js builds (webapp/public/libav/, built by webapp/libav/build.sh): every module and
its licence. FFmpeg 9.0 is configured without --enable-gpl, --enable-version3 or --enable-nonfree
(its configure says "License: LGPL version 2.1 or later" for both builds), so no GPL part is in.
The full LGPL text is LGPL-2.1.txt beside this file; the exact sources are served at /libav/sources/.
