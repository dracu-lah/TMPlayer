TMPlayer is a player for the videos already in your own Telegram chats and channels. Sign in once and watch them on an Android TV or Google TV, a Fire TV stick, an Android phone or tablet, or a Windows, macOS or Linux computer. It is free, open source and unofficial.

A video starts within seconds while it is still arriving, so a large file never has to download first, and seeking jumps straight to the part you asked for.

On a TV:
- Sign in by scanning a QR code with the phone you already use for Telegram.
- Everything works with the remote: rows for Continue watching and your starred chats, search across all your chats, and a player that keeps the picture clear.
- Runs on Android TV and Google TV boxes, Chromecast with Google TV, NVIDIA Shield, Mi Box, projectors and Fire TV.

On a phone:
- Stream Telegram videos without downloading them first, or download a whole season for the train.
- Picture in picture, background audio, gestures for seeking, volume and brightness, and a screen lock for small hands.
- Low storage mode keeps only the part of a video you are watching, for phones that are always full.

Everywhere:
- MKV, MP4, HEVC, AV1 and more, with subtitles from the file, from a file of your own or found online, and every audio track the file carries.
- Resume where you stopped, series pages that put episodes in order, skip intro and next episode, and course progress for numbered lessons.
- Night mode for sound keeps dialogue clear and loud scenes quiet; volume boost for quiet files.
- Videos posted in pieces play as one video; archives that cannot be played are listed with the reason.
- Light and Dark themes with a Pure black switch for OLED screens, nine accent colours, and 22 languages.
- No ads of its own, no analytics, no server run by the developer. TMPlayer talks to Telegram directly through TDLib, Telegram's own library, and your data stays on your devices.

TMPlayer only plays what is already in your chats. Use it with videos you are allowed to watch.
