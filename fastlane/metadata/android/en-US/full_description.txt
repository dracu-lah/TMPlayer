TMPlayer plays the videos in your own Telegram chats and channels on an Android TV, a phone, or a Windows or Linux computer. It starts within seconds while the file is still downloading, and seeking re-aims the download instead of waiting for it.

Sign in by scanning a QR code with your phone, pick a chat, and only its playable videos appear. No messages, no server, no account except yours.

Features:
- **Instant Playback**: Start watching within seconds, even for large files.
- **Secure**: Communicates directly with Telegram's servers. No third-party analytics or developer-operated backends.
- **Cross-Platform**: Available for Android TV, mobile, Windows, and Linux.
- **Open Source**: Fully open-source and transparent.

TMPlayer is an unofficial client. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Telegram FZ-LLC.
