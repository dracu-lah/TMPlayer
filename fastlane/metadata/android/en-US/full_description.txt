TMPlayer is a player for the videos already in your own Telegram chats and channels. Sign in once and watch them on an Android TV or Google TV, a Fire TV stick, an Android phone or tablet, or a Windows, macOS or Linux computer. It is free, open source and unofficial.

A video starts within seconds while it is still arriving, so a large file never has to download first, and seeking jumps straight to the part you asked for.

Easy to start:
- Sign in with your phone number, or on a TV by scanning a QR code with the phone you already use for Telegram.
- One question after sign in: which chats hold your videos. Everything else is chosen for your device and can be changed later in Settings.

On a phone:
- Stream without downloading first, or download a whole season. The Downloads tab shows what is running, paused or saved, and Free up space gives room back without touching Telegram.
- While streaming, choose Save space (keeps only the part you are watching, for phones that are always full) or Keep video (keeps the whole file, so going back is instant).
- Offline, Home puts what is on your phone first and says what needs the internet.
- Picture in picture, background audio, gestures for seeking, volume and brightness, and a screen lock for small hands.

On a TV:
- Everything works with the remote: rows for Continue watching and your starred chats, search across all your chats, and a player that keeps the picture clear.
- Built for weak sticks: a held arrow stops when you let go and a scrub makes one seek, even on 1 GB of memory.
- Profiles with a PIN and a quick sign out suit a TV the family shares.
- Runs on Android TV and Google TV boxes, Chromecast with Google TV, NVIDIA Shield, Mi Box, projectors and Fire TV.

Series and anime:
- Series pages put every season and episode in order, with season packs and episode ranges understood, and a button to resume the episode you were on.
- Anime gets its own episode titles and pictures, even when the files count episodes straight through.
- Skip intro, recap and credits buttons use the chapters inside the file, then community timing databases, then your own mark. Choose per kind: ask, skip by itself, or off.

Playing well:
- MKV, MP4, HEVC, AV1 and more, with subtitles from the file, from a file of your own or found online, and every audio track the file carries.
- Small badges show 4K, 1080p, HDR, Dolby Vision, Atmos or 5.1 where the file says so.
- If the picture does not start or freezes while the sound goes on, TMPlayer tries another decoder or player by itself, and a Report stutter button marks the moment for the logs.
- Night mode for sound keeps dialogue clear and loud scenes quiet; volume boost for quiet files.
- A sleep timer, A-B repeat, bookmarks, a speed remembered for each show, and Save to a folder with the free space shown.
- Light, Dark and Pure black themes for OLED screens, and 22 languages.
- Videos posted in pieces play as one video; archives that cannot be played are listed with the reason.

Keep my place on all my devices:
- Optional, off until you say yes. Where you stopped, what you watched, Watch later, favourites, bookmarks and the titles you fixed are kept in one small file in your own Saved Messages, so your phone, TV and computer agree. There is no TMPlayer server in between.

Free, with no ads of its own, no analytics and no server run by the developer. TMPlayer talks to Telegram directly through TDLib, Telegram's own library, and your data stays on your devices. Optional lookups for posters, subtitles and skip times send only a title or an id, never your videos or anything from Telegram. If it is useful to you, you can support it through Buy Me a Coffee or UPI; nothing is locked behind it.

TMPlayer only plays what is already in your chats. Use it with videos you are allowed to watch.
