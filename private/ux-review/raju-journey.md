# Raju's journey: a common man who downloads and plays Telegram videos

2026-11-16. Read-only review (no source edited). Persona and steps: `private/UX-PERSONA-JOURNEY.md`;
bar and D1 to D8: `private/UX-INTERACTION-PLAN.md`. Earlier reviews are cited by id (ON-n, PL-n from
`private/UX-REVIEW-2026-11-15.md`) and not repeated in full.

## How this was done, and how far each claim can be trusted

- **seen on phone** = driven on the POCO M2 Pro (build 1.28.6-dev, the user's signed-in account) with
  adb screenshots; the picture is `design/ux-review/raju/<name>.webp` (WebP q88). The account was
  already signed in, so steps 1 to 3 (first open, sign in, setup) could NOT be seen on the phone:
  those are **from code** (copy in `core/src/commonMain/resources/i18n/en.json`, the earlier review)
  and from desktop render shots.
- **from code** = read in source or en.json, not verified on a device.
- **render** = a desktop/TV render PNG from `TMPLAYER_SHOTS=<dir> ./gradlew :desktop:test` (scratch only).
- Web app: the e2e server with its fake Telegram, headless Chromium at 390 wide (scratch only).
- The Android `*RenderTest` run was started and stopped (machine busy with other workers); the phone
  replaced it. TV is code, render and the 11-15 Mi TV pass only.
- Phone state put back: rotation (`wm user-rotation free`, accelerometer on), Wi-Fi on, mini player
  closed, the one download I started (Resident Evil, 5 percent) was cancelled. Raju's own paused
  "The Mongoose" download and its resume point were not touched. One side effect: Jailer 2 was played
  for about 20 s, so it may show in History > Recent.
- Each finding is marked **patch** (small, fix in place) or **redesign** (structural; the note under
  "Redesign proposals" gives problem, new flow on all platforms, and what it deletes).

Severity: **blocker** = Raju cannot do the job or thinks the app is broken; **annoying** = he gets
there with doubt or extra taps; **polish** = looks or reads wrong but does not stop him.

## Top 10 reasons Raju would quit

1. **His videos are not there.** The default 400 MB floor hides every ordinary clip, and the only clue is a "1 hidden" chip (4.1). He concludes the app cannot see his videos.
2. **The setup asks six questions before the first video** (3.1): themes, posters, performance, crash reports, storage. He taps Next without reading and is left with settings he does not understand.
3. **Offline, the banner covers the tabs** (9.1) and every card still says Play (9.2): the app looks dead exactly when only downloads can work.
4. **Downloads and Settings are behind his own profile photo** (7.1, PL-16). He looks for a Downloads tab, does not find it, and starts the same download twice.
5. **No number on a download from the video's own page** (5.2): a hairline ring and a toast. With a 2.6 GB file and mobile data, he does not know the cost before or the progress after.
6. **Six unlabelled icons in the player** (6.2) and no skip buttons: he cannot find subtitles or sound, and Back leaves the film playing in a mini bar (6.4).
7. **Jargon at the worst moment**: "proxy", "Save logs", "TDLib", "stack trace", "Two-step verification" (2.2, 3.2, 9.3, 9.4).
8. **Storage settings read like an engineer's panel**: seven switches, two of them twins (8.1). He leaves defaults and then cannot stop mobile data use with confidence.
9. **Typing the words he thinks in finds nothing in Settings** (11.2: "bigger", "font", "log out"), so "make text bigger" feels impossible.
10. **The floating bar hides content and hides itself** (4.5, 7.3): captions and the empty state's buttons sit under it; at the end of Home there is no bar; on the web two bars stack (4.10).

## Redesign proposals

Each stops at this note for the user's OK when big (a new Home, merged screens); the rest can go ahead.
Platform columns: phone / TV remote and focus / desktop keyboard / web.

### R1. Show every video by default (replaces the 400 MB floor and its dialog)

- **Problem.** `SizeFilter.DEFAULT_MIN = 400 MB` hides ordinary clips for everyone; the cue is a "1 hidden" chip (4.1).
- **New flow.** Default minimum 0 (`SizeFilter.FLOOR`). In a chat's Sort sheet one switch "Hide small clips" (under 50 MB, `CLIP_MIN`), off by default. When on, the grid ends with a row "3 small videos hidden. Show them" (one tap turns the switch off for this visit). Home rows keep their own sensible floor so trailers do not fill Recently added; say so in the row's "See all" page.
- **Platforms.** Phone: switch in `VideoSortSheet.kt`, end row in the grid. TV: same sheet, D-pad Down to the switch, focus stays on it after toggling, end row focusable. Desktop: same sheet; Tab reaches the switch and the end row. Web: parity gap already listed in `docs/ux-audit.md` (no sort filters on web); add the end row with the same wording, default 0 (the e2e shots already set `sizeMin: 0`).
- **Deletes.** `settings.smallest*` radio dialog, `SizeQuickSheet.kt`, the Settings min and max sliders, the "1 hidden" chip, `state.empty_hidden` copy that quotes 400 MB; keep the stored `min_size_bytes` readable (migrate: if it equals 400 MB treat as default off).
- **Edge cases.** A user who set a floor on purpose keeps it (stored value other than default). 100,000 videos: counting hidden is one query (`SizeFilter` already counts "1 hidden"). Notifications (`NewVideoAlerts`, `EpisodeAlerts`) use `minSizeBytes`: keep their own 50 MB so a clip does not ping.

### R2. Downloads as a first-class place (and one honest storage page)

- **Problem.** Downloads is behind the profile photo (7.1, PL-16), has two lists for one idea (7.4), and its settings are seven switches (8.1).
- **New flow.** Tab bar: Home, Chats, Search, **Downloads** (badge = running). Profile and Settings become a gear in Home's top bar and the Downloads page header. On the page: running cards (current design is good), one list "On this phone" (downloaded and auto-saved together; auto-saved wear a small "Auto-saved" tag and a "Keep" action), "Free up space" only when useful. Settings "Downloads and data" page as in 8.1: Where, "On mobile data: Ask first / Never / Allow", "While streaming: Keep video / Save space", "Remove after watching".
- **Platforms.** Phone: 4 tab floating bar. TV: top bar item "Downloads" next to Home, Search, Chats; face keeps Settings; Back from the player returns focus to the opener. Desktop: sidebar item with Ctrl+J and the same list, Delete and Ctrl+A already in `GridSelection.kt`. Web: no downloads (needs app): keep the "Get the app" card in the same slot.
- **Deletes.** The avatar menu entries Downloads/History/Watch later as separate doors can stay under "You", but Downloads leaves it; `downloads.cached*` section and header, `settings.save_mobile_data`, `settings.wifi_only` (migrated into the 3 way), `settings.alert_low_storage` and `settings.pause_downloads_while_playing` switches (always on).
- **Edge cases.** Stored booleans map: wifi_only true -> Never on mobile data; false and save_mobile_data true -> Ask first; both false -> Ask first (the safe default). Huge lists: virtualised, as today. Cancel gets Undo (7.2).

### R3. A one question setup

- **Problem.** Four pages plus crash question plus gate plus tour before the first video (3.1).
- **New flow.** After sign in: "Which chats have your videos?" (list sorted by video count, "120 videos" under each name, search box, nothing ticked, Done disabled until one is, Back asks "Leave without choosing?"). Then Home. DeviceFit sets Lighter or Full silently; crash reports default off; the tour is a "Tips" card on Home that can be dismissed for good (not a modal). Settings > "Redo setup choices" stays.
- **Platforms.** Phone and desktop: the picker as is, simplified. TV: focus starts on the first chat, OK ticks, Right moves to Done, Back asks to leave. Web: already one step (`/setup/chats`).
- **Deletes.** `SetupScreen` pages (Appearance, Posters, Performance, Storage/mobile/crash), `setup.*` strings that no page uses (the dead ones in ON-19 too), the modal tour offer (`tour.offer_*`), the crash ask card after skip (`setup.crash_ask_*`: ask once in Settings, Privacy).
- **Edge cases.** No video chats (ON-4): show Saved Messages and the "Forward a video to Saved Messages" hint. Slow chat list (ON-7): text under the spinner with `picker.loading`. Stop if offline: show the offline card (R4).

### R4. Offline mode that tells the truth

- **Problem.** Offline, every card says Play, the banner covers the tabs (9.1, 9.2).
- **New flow.** Offline: Home shows "On this phone" first (downloads and recently played), other cards greyed with "Needs internet" and no Play; the hero is replaced by the first downloaded video; the banner sits above the tab bar, small, with "Try again". A tap on a greyed card opens its page with the one line "Connect to play, or download it first".
- **Platforms.** Phone and desktop: as above. TV: focus skips greyed cards? No: focusable but OK shows the line; first focus lands on a playable card. Web: no downloads; show the Reload card (`web.stopped_loading`).
- **Deletes.** The `mainConnectToPlay` toast path for Home cards, the passive Offline pill duplication, PL-13's separate fix.
- **Edge cases.** Flaky link flipping online and offline: debounce 2 s so cards do not flash; a resume of a fully cached video must start at once (PL-14).

### R5. Player controls with words (patch scale, but one shared overlay)

- Labels under the six icons for the first 3 sessions (then icons only), plus back 10 and forward 10 beside play; the audio icon answers when there is one track (6.2, 6.3). TV: same labels, the row remembers the last focused button (PL-8). Desktop: the same overlay, tooltips with keys already in `player.*_hint`. Not a redesign of layout, so it goes ahead with a render test per form factor.
