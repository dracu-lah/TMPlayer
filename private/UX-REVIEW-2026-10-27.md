# TMPlayer UX review, 2026-10-27

A screen by screen read of TMPlayer as a demanding viewer used to Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+,
YouTube, Plex, Stremio and Infuse would meet it today. It sits beside
[OTT-REVIEW.md](OTT-REVIEW.md) (2026-10-19), which lists the missing features (Next up, mark season
watched, desktop scrub frames, profiles, OS integration). None of those are repeated here: this one
is about the experience of what already exists: friction, confusion, waits, wording, layout, focus
and states that differ between platforms.

Inputs: screenshots taken today on the POCO phone, the TV emulator, the desktop build on sway and
the web sign-in in headless Chromium (scratchpad `dev/p-*`, `dev/tv-*`, `desk1-3`, `car5-6`,
`web-*`), and the code at `d6e8637`. In-app labels are quoted as they are ("Film", "Movies").

**Value** is how much a viewer notices: High, Medium, Low. **Cost**: S (a day or less), M (a few
days), L (a week or more). **Status**: Fixed `<hash>`, In progress (another agent), Proposed.

**In progress elsewhere, not planned here:** History removal with Undo, checkboxes and one click
Clear history with the plain "Remove from history" wording; Home row switches and chosen chats for
Recently added. **Waiting for the user's go:** the size filter default back to 400 MB (50 MB
today) with a visible "n hidden" chip that opens a quick modal.
