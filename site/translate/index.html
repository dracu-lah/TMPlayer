Help translate TMPlayer

Fix a word

Three ways, from the quickest to the most useful.

  1. Tell us

    Say which page or screen, what it says and what it should say, in the Telegram group or in an issue on GitHub. A screenshot helps.

  2. Edit the file yourself

    Every language is one JSON file. The website's are in site/i18n/, the app's in core/src/commonMain/resources/i18n/, each named after its code, such as es-419.json. Press the pencil on GitHub, change the text, and propose the change as a pull request.

    English is the source: en.json is never translated, only the other files are.

  3. Review a whole language

    Read the site or the app in your language from start to finish and send one list of fixes. A reviewed language is marked as reviewed, and later machine runs never overwrite it.

A few rules

Keep the braces

Words in curly braces, such as {version} or {a1}, are filled in by the app or the site. Leave them in, in whatever order your grammar needs.

Keep the names

TMPlayer, Telegram, Android TV, Fire TV, Windows, Linux and GitHub stay as they are.

Short beats literal

A button that fits is better than a perfect sentence that wraps. Say it the way the people you know would say it.

A new language

Open an issue naming the language and saying whether you can review it. A language goes on the site once almost all of it is translated.
