A player, pointed at an account you already have

Telegram is where a lot of people keep the things they mean to watch later, and then watch on a phone held at arm's length because getting the file onto the television is more trouble than the film is worth. TMPlayer is the missing half of that: a Telegram media player that signs in as you, lists the videos in your chats, and plays them on the big screen without moving a file anywhere.

What it does

Streams while it downloads, seeks properly, plays MKV, MP4, AVI and TS with subtitles and several audio tracks, and starts the next episode on its own.

The full list

On Android TV

A ten-foot layout built for a remote: a navigation rail, cards you can read across a room, focus you can always see, and a QR code instead of typing a phone number.

Made for the television

What it is not

Not a media service, not a channel directory, not a Telegram client. It hosts nothing, recommends nothing, and cannot read or send a message.

Questions and limits

Three things, once

Then it is a television app like any other.

  1. Get the APK

    One universal file for every supported device, television or phone, 32-bit and 64-bit alike. There is no chip type to look up and nothing to pay.

    Go to the download page

  2. Sideload it

    On a phone you open the file and say yes when Android asks. On a television it is a free helper app from the Play Store, or adb from a computer on the same wifi.

    Read the four install steps

  3. Scan the QR code

    Telegram's own Link Desktop Device flow, signed in as you through Telegram's official library. No account of mine, no bot, and nothing in between.

    What that does and does not mean
