TMPlayer Your Telegram films, on the TV.

The eight gigabyte file stays where it is. The TV plays it from there.

Sign in to your own Telegram account by scanning a QR code, then play the videos already sitting in your chats. Playback starts while the file is still arriving, and seeking re-aims the download instead of waiting for it. Built and tested on a Mi TV Stick with 1 GB of RAM.

      How to install
      
          
      Four steps. You only do them once.
      
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
        
          
      1. 
            
        Allow apps from outside the store
        
            
        On the TV, open Settings, then Device Preferences, then About, and press Build seven times to turn on Developer options. Then enable Unknown sources (or Install unknown apps) for whichever sideloading app you use.
        
          
        2. 
          
      2. 
            
        Get the APK onto the TV
        
            
        The tidiest way is adb from a computer on the same network. Turn on network debugging in Developer options, then:
        
            
        adb connect <tv-ip>:5555
adb install TMPlayer-<version>-armeabi-v7a.apk
        
            
        No computer to hand? A sideload helper from the Play Store (Downloader, Send Files to TV, or similar) can fetch the same file straight from the download link above.
        
          
        3. 
          
      3. 
            
        Sign in by QR code
        
            
        Open TMPlayer. It draws a QR code. On the phone that has your Telegram, go to Settings, then Devices, then Link Desktop Device, and point the camera at the TV. No typing an email address with a D-pad. Two-step verification works.
        
          
        4. 
          
      4. 
            
        Pick a chat and press play
        
            
        Your chats appear as poster grids of the videos inside them. Choose one and it starts in a few seconds.
        
          
        5. 
        
      
        
      INSTALL.md covers the sideloading steps in full, plus the remote-control reference and troubleshooting.
      
      
      
    
      
  
      

  
      
    
      
      
      
        
      
          
      Be clear about what this is
      
          
      A player pointed at an account you already have.
      
        
      
      
      

      
      
        
      
          
      What it is
      
          
        
            
      • A player for your own Telegram account, signed in with your own credentials through TDLib, Telegram's official client library.
        • 
            
      • A way to watch the videos already in chats and channels you have joined, without downloading them to a phone first.
        • 
            
      • Free and open source under Apache-2.0. You can read every line, build it yourself, and change it.
        • 
          
      
        
      
        
      
          
      What it is not
      
          
        
            
      • Not a content service. It hosts nothing, indexes nothing, and uploads nothing. There is no server and no bot.
        • 
            
      • Not a general Telegram client. You cannot read messages, reply, or send anything.
        • 
            
      • Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Telegram FZ-LLC. What you watch, and where it came from, is your responsibility.
