TMPlayer project information

Lawful use

TMPlayer is a media player for videos already accessible through your own Telegram account. It does not provide media or access to channels.

Bring your own authorized media

Use TMPlayer only with content you own, content you have permission to view, or content otherwise available to you under applicable law. TMPlayer does not grant rights to any media shown through the app.

The app does not host, upload, sell, index, recommend, or discover media or Telegram channels. It does not bypass Telegram permissions, paywalls, encryption, or digital access controls.

Rights-holder contact

If you believe a specific image, file, or statement published on the TMPlayer website or GitHub repository infringes your rights, email nevilnicks4321@gmail.com. Identify the exact URL or repository path, the work concerned, and how you are authorized to act. Reports about media inside a Telegram chat should be directed to Telegram or the party that posted it, because TMPlayer does not possess that media.
