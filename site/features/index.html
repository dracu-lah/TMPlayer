What a Telegram video player has to get right

Playing a file is the easy half. Here is the rest, on the TV, the phone and the desktop.

Download How to install

What it does

Built for a remote, a thumb, and a mouse and keyboard.

Watching

  • Starts in seconds and seeks properly. Skipping ahead re-aims the download.
  • Plays real-world files. MKV, MP4, AVI and TS, with subtitles and audio tracks.
  • Remembers where you stopped, with a Continue watching row on launch.
  • Keeps one video on the device. The previous one is cleared, so an 8 GB stick never fills up.
  • Is a phone app on a phone. The same library, drawn for the hand.

Browsing

  • Shows media, not messages. Chats filter down to playable videos, including videos sent as files.
  • Understands episode filenames. The next one starts after a countdown you can stop.
  • Forgives how you type. Typos, missing accents and one wrong word still find the video.
  • Search by voice with your remote's microphone, and star chats to keep them one press away.
  • Updates itself on Android from GitHub. Signing out clears everything it kept.

What it looks like

Four screens in the order you meet them, filled with demo content.

On Windows and Linux

The same account, library and streaming as the TV, in a window built for mouse and keyboard. macOS later.

The window

  • Signs in with the same QR code, or with a phone number.
  • A sidebar and a grid. Chats, Favourites, Continue, Downloads and Settings, with posters you can resize.
  • Right click a video to play, restart, download or copy its Telegram link.
  • Downloads stay on the computer until you remove them.
  • Remembers its window size and position. A second launch brings it forward.

The player

  • Played through mpv: MKV, MP4, AVI, TS, image subtitles, DTS, TrueHD and E-AC3, all bundled.
  • Streams while it downloads and seeks without waiting.
  • Picks up where you stopped, offers the next episode, and remembers subtitle and audio choices per series.
  • Keeps the screen awake while a video plays.
  • Answers the media keys, and the Linux desktop's media controls.
